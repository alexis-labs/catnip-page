1. Who we are

Catnip Money is a mobile game for Android. This Privacy Policy applies to the Catnip Money game, its related Google Play listing, and this privacy policy website.

Replace this placeholder before publishing with the legal name of the developer, studio, or company responsible for the game: [Developer / Company Name].

2. Data we collect

Catnip Money is designed to collect as little personal data as possible. Depending on the features you use, the game may collect or process the following data:

  • Game progress stored on your device: coins, upgrades, settings, achievements, and other gameplay state needed to continue your game.
  • Leaderboard information, if enabled: a player display name or alias, score, rank, leaderboard entries, submission time, and an internal player identifier.
  • Technical data needed to run online features: basic request data such as IP address, timestamps, and service logs may be processed by our backend provider when the game connects to online services.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, we process the email address and information you choose to include so we can respond.
  • Public market data: if the game displays cryptocurrency or market reference data through CoinGecko or a similar provider, the game may request that information from the provider. We do not use this to identify you.

Catnip Money does not ask for sensitive personal information such as government ID, payment card details, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or health information.

3. How we use data

We use data only for game-related and support purposes, including to:

  • save and restore local game progress on your device;
  • operate optional leaderboard features and show rankings;
  • prevent obvious abuse of online leaderboard submissions;
  • keep backend services reliable, secure, and working correctly;
  • respond to support or privacy questions;
  • display public market reference data if that feature is included in the game.

We do not use personal data for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising profiling unless this policy is updated and the game is changed to clearly support that use.

4. Local storage and game progress on device

Most game progress is stored locally on your Android device. This may include coins, upgrades, unlocked items, preferences, and gameplay statistics.

Local progress is normally not sent to us unless an online feature, such as a leaderboard, requires a score submission or you choose to send information to us for support. If you uninstall the game, clear app data, or change devices, locally stored progress may be deleted and may not be recoverable.

5. Leaderboard and optional backend usage

Catnip Money may include optional online features such as leaderboards. If you use these features, the game may send the data required to operate them to a backend service. This may include a display name or alias, score, rank, submission time, and an internal player identifier.

We may use Supabase as the backend provider for leaderboard storage, authentication features if later added, database hosting, and service logs. Supabase may process technical information such as IP address, request metadata, and timestamps as part of providing the service.

Leaderboard entries may be visible to other players if the leaderboard is public. Do not use your real name or share personal information in a display name.

6. Third-party services

Catnip Money may use third-party services that process data according to their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Play: distributes the Android app and may process account, device, crash, install, purchase, and usage information under Google's policies.
  • Supabase: may host optional leaderboard or backend data and related technical logs if online features are enabled.
  • CoinGecko or similar market data providers: may provide public market reference data used inside the game. These requests are not intended to identify individual players.

Before publishing, remove any service listed here that the game does not actually use, and add any service that the game does use, such as analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or cloud save providers.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

We may share or disclose data only in the following limited situations:

  • with service providers that help operate the game, such as backend hosting;
  • when required to comply with law, legal process, or enforceable government request;
  • to protect the rights, safety, and security of players, the game, or our services;
  • as part of a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of the game, if applicable.

8. Data retention

Local game progress remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, uninstall the game, or the game replaces it during normal operation.

Leaderboard and backend records are kept for as long as needed to operate the online feature, maintain ranking history, prevent abuse, resolve support requests, and meet legal or security needs. We will delete or anonymize backend records when they are no longer needed for these purposes.

Support emails are retained for as long as needed to handle your request and maintain reasonable records of our response.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data processed by Catnip Money, including limiting the data collected, using reputable service providers, and relying on secure connections where supported.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe there is a security issue affecting Catnip Money, please contact us at scribbworks@gmail.com.

10. Children's privacy

Catnip Money is not intended to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children or knowingly sell or share children's personal information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us at scribbworks@gmail.com and we will take reasonable steps to review and delete the information where appropriate.

11. International transfers

Catnip Money and its service providers may process data in countries other than the country where you live. These countries may have different data protection laws. When we use service providers, we expect them to protect data according to their contractual obligations and applicable law.

12. User rights and in-app data deletion

Catnip Money is designed to provide data deletion controls inside the application. Use the in-game privacy or settings controls to delete local game progress or request removal of online data associated with optional features such as leaderboards.

You can also delete local game progress by using Android's app data controls, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the game. Deleting local app data may not delete leaderboard entries that were already submitted to an online backend, so use the in-app deletion flow for online records when available.

For privacy questions that are not covered by the in-app controls, contact scribbworks@gmail.com.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or security concerns, contact: scribbworks@gmail.com.

14. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game changes, when we add or remove services, or when legal or platform requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last changed.

If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to make the updated policy available through this page, the Google Play listing, or inside the game where appropriate.
